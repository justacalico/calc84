# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

CALC-84 is a graphing calculator. It does not collect, transmit, or share any
personal data.

## Data collection

None. The app makes no network requests, contains no analytics, no advertising
SDKs, no crash reporters, and no account system. Everything you type into the
calculator stays on your device.

## Local storage

The app stores the following on your device only:

- Calculation history and equation entries
- Lists, matrices, programs, and variables you save
- Settings such as angle mode and display format

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app
or clear its storage.

## Permissions

The app requests no permissions on any platform. Release builds declare no
Android permissions at all.

## Third parties

No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected. The app
bundles no third-party tracking or advertising libraries.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone,
including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be committed to this file and
noted in the release notes.
